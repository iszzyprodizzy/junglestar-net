Why IFPs / RFPs belong here

Old paperwork becomes reusable intelligence.

🏛️

2023 IFP

Not “old junk.” It can preserve agency language, eligibility rules, scoring criteria, required attachments, project descriptions, budget assumptions, deadlines, contacts and what you already wrote once.

📑

New RFP

DocuBot can compare the new solicitation against prior language and flag what is reusable, what changed, what is missing and what must be newly sourced.

🧾

Machinery / Equipment Appraisal

Useful as evidence and asset inventory: item, manufacturer/model/serial, condition, valuation date, appraised value, source photos, location, ownership and the matter/project it supports.

🍽️

Restaurant / Licensing

Applications become a reusable launch checklist: agency, permit/license, required documents, fee, inspection, submission date, approval date, renewals, deficiencies and next action.
