01 / Our creative legacy

We didn't start with a pitch. We started creating.

Long before the food heroes, our founder spent approximately 15 years developing the JW Comics universe. That foundation expanded into children's publishing, original music, games, maker activities and educational storytelling.

From a small early food venture and hands-on commercial experience to printing and bookbinding, Jungle Star's history is one of creating with available tools—and bringing other authors, including young writers and older adults, into the publishing process.

The work already exists

Published books. Original characters. Recorded music. Creative production. A playable game proof-of-concept. Now building the business infrastructure to expand their reach.

02 / Four operations · one connected mission

Real businesses. Connected impact.

Our development initiative links food enterprise, food production, educational recreation, and adult creative commerce. Revenue-generating food operations lead the startup sequence, supporting the long-term growth of our family and community spaces.

01
First economic engine

Isaac's Phried Chicken

From a $40 grill to a recognized Philadelphia food enterprise, Isaac's Phried Chicken was built through years of discipline, food-service experience, and hard-won growth. During the 2015 Sharswood–Blumberg condemnation period, the founder reports an approximately $90,000 commercial-equipment appraisal. The next chapter brings that experience back to market through food carts, mobile service, catering and future storefronts—built to generate sales, jobs and momentum.

Development allocation$175,000
02
Food production & distribution

Community Food Processing & Meal Center

Our culinary and food-production hub connects the restaurant, neighborhood meal service, and local agriculture. We plan to prepare restaurant ingredients, train culinary workers, package meals for customers and community distribution, and produce seasonings, pickled peppers, Barker Family Brew teas and juices. Our sourcing philosophy puts nearby farms, gardens and growers first, expanding outward when local supply cannot meet demand. We build a better world one location—and one supply chain—at a time.

Development allocation$250,000
03
Children & families

Little Legends Arcade & Academy

What began as a heartfelt song from a father to his son is growing into a place where imagination becomes opportunity. Little Legends brings stories to life through games, reading, 3D printing, robotics, school visits, creative parties and hands-on STEM experiences. Children come to play, discover what they love, and leave with the confidence to build something of their own.

Development allocation$250,000
04
Adults, makers & community

Jungle Star City

Imagine the excitement of a major arcade and entertainment destination—with a makerspace where adults can turn curiosity into intellectual property, marketable skills and new businesses. Jungle Star City brings games, music, performance, fabrication, embroidery, robotics and creative production together. Each station is designed to connect the fun to practical skills, entrepreneurship and local impact, supported by the Community Housing & Occupational Alliance's resource-navigation mission.

03 / The sustainable economic engine

Revenue that powers lasting opportunity.

Our model combines everyday sales with long-term creative intellectual property. Food, books, games, events, makerspaces, manufacturing and services form connected sources of earned revenue.

Start where demand is immediate.

A licensed food-cart or mobile food pilot can be a first-stage commercial operation, supplied by an approved kitchen. We will evaluate cart, truck, shared-kitchen and dedicated-kitchen routes as capital and locations develop.

Food products include Isaac's Phried Chicken, prepared meals, seasonings, Barker Family Brew teas and juices, and other approved packaged products.

Grow the ecosystem.

Children's school visits, parties, arcade admissions and maker workshops create educational revenue. Adult events, design services, printing, embroidery, 3D toys, robotics products, comics, soundtracks and games expand the commercial base.

Jungle Star doesn't just teach people to fish. We teach them to make the rod, irrigate the water, cultivate the ecosystem, bring their products to market, and sustain the enterprises they create. A book, a meal, a toy, or a new skill can become the beginning of a livelihood.

How we count the cost and grow in stages

“For which of you, intending to build a tower, sitteth not down first, and counteth the cost, whether he have sufficient to finish it?” — Luke 14:28 (KJV). That is the principle behind our stage-by-stage development and investment in operations that can support themselves.

Our development planning starts with the food-service unit economics: setup costs, approved preparation space, daily sales volume, ingredient cost, labor, insurance, location economics and cash reserves. Each later operation will have its own feasibility study, floor plan, staffing model, operating budget and revenue targets.

Current planning stages include an initial $10,000 development milestone, a broader $500,000 founder development campaign, and a $1 million four-operation initiative. These stages organize different scopes of work, not additional amounts automatically added to the same construction budget.

04 / Founder's reinvestment commitment

Success should return to the communities that make it possible.

The Jungle Star Creative Reinvestment Model
Jungle Star Kids is a mission of community reinvestment. Our founder has designated 100% of his personal proceeds from his Jungle Star Kids creative work—including the The Melodies of a Farmer educational series—toward children’s education, healthy living, creative opportunity and community development. What began with a song for his son is meant to keep giving back to children everywhere.

His continuing goal is to match outside contributions to Jungle Star's community-development mission dollar for dollar through eligible personal proceeds as they are earned and available—without a fixed overall matching ceiling or a restriction to one prize or program.

Those matching contributions are intended to help develop the specific schools, learning programs, community operations and opportunities supporters make possible. Additional JW Comics and Jungle Star intellectual properties have their own founder-directed giving commitments.

Faithful over a few things. Building toward opportunities for many. — Matthew 25:21

Two distinct Peace Prize initiatives

Jungle Star Kids Peace Prize — championing children, young creators, healthy living, education and service.

Jungle Star Peace Prize — honoring creators, educators, inventors and peacemakers making a lasting difference.

Our long-range vision is a permanent, professionally administered legacy fund supported by designated proceeds from Jungle Star enterprises. Inspired by enduring prize institutions, its goal is to preserve capital, grow community investment and support future generations. The fund will be established with its own stewardship, governance and financial structure.

Philadelphia first. The world next.

Our purpose begins in Philadelphia neighborhoods, grows across underserved communities in the United States, and ultimately reaches communities around the world.

Our mission is designed to endure beyond one founder through lasting enterprises, leadership, stewardship and future generations.
