# Jungle Star reusable content templates — v1

These are **starter templates**, not live pages. They are kept separate so a page can be reviewed before replacing a public route.

## Shared stylesheet
- `/assets/css/content-system.css`
- The stylesheet is intentionally opt-in. Existing pages keep their current look until someone adds the stylesheet and the `js-*` classes.
- It includes responsive layout, visible keyboard focus, accessible form styling, and print rules.

## Templates
- `vox-article.html` — sourced article, reporting notes, citations, corrections.
- `book-club-session.html` — reusable reading/discussion session.
- `character-profile.html` — character canon, appearances, media and commerce links.
- `research-record.html` — research question, method, evidence, limitations and findings.

## Safe publishing path
1. Copy a template into a new draft file.
2. Replace every bracketed placeholder.
3. Confirm image and story rights, sources, dates, consent, and public links.
4. Check mobile layout, keyboard navigation, link destinations, and print preview.
5. Publish only after review.

## Evidence and privacy rules
- Distinguish verified facts, interviewee statements, editorial interpretation, and plans.
- Do not describe self-selected questionnaire answers or street interviews as representative of a whole population.
- Do not publish private contact information or identifying information about children without appropriate authorization.
- A visible HTML form is not a working data collection system until a secure, consent-aware backend is connected.
- Use real URLs; do not leave `href="#"` as a pretend destination.
