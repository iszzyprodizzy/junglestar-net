Full sponsor disclosure

What your support means

Jungle Star develops games, books, music, technology, creative learning, and community programs. Funding may support the specific purposes described in each campaign or sponsorship agreement. Project recognition, NPC references, billboards, game credits, website acknowledgments, event access, and story integration require separate approval and are not guaranteed. Creative direction and youth safety come first.

Sponsorship is not equity, ownership, investment, or profit share. Payments are not represented as tax-deductible charitable donations. The founder's personal-assistance channel is separate from company revenue and restricted project support. We will publish budgets, milestones, and verified impact reporting as systems become available; future impact dashboards are a plan, not an existing reporting service.

For partnerships, in-kind equipment, volunteering, or financial questions, contact Team@JungleStar.app.
