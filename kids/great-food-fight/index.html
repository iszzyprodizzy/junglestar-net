01 / See what we've built

A world you can already play.

From an idea to a playable demo—in the same year.

The Great Food Fight's 2025 demo brought original food heroes and villains into a playable world. The current proof of concept establishes a foundation for an evolving family game, a larger Philadelphia adventure and sponsor-supported development.

General Broccoli and the garden defenders face junk-food monsters, while the wider game design brings food science, practical skills, reading, building and neighborhood service into the action.

04 / Sponsor a mission. Help build the world.

Your profession could become their next adventure.

Food & farms

Neighborhood business

Become Part of the City

Approved in-world storefronts, billboards, local-business missions and community experiences can introduce players to real professions.

Where development support goes

A scoped sponsor mission can fund creative production, design, programming, testing, artwork, audio and the shared systems that make the feature work. Each paid integration receives a written scope, recognition terms and development budget.

About the growing mission library

The founder's creative archive contains multiple expanding mission indexes and performance-script collections. The supplied Master Mission Outline v3 is explicitly organized as 318 numbered missions; the broader ongoing archive is described by the founder as 400+ outlines and approximately 421 mission ideas. These are story and design materials—not a claim that hundreds of playable missions are already coded.

Story canon remains organized separately from flexible online community and sponsor missions, allowing the larger city experience to grow without rewriting the main narrative.
