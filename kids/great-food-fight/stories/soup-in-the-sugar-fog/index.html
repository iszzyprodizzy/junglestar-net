FOUNDER APPROVED • PERFORMANCE SCRIPT 007

SOUP IN THE SUGAR FOG

Hope Enters With a Spoon

New performance draft built from recovered canon material.

Opening

NARRATOR: Yesterday, they mapped the fog. Today, they have to go back. Not with swords. Not with cannons. Not with a legendary vegetable army. With soup. Water. Food bags. Pamphlets. Resource cards. And one very important rule: If you're going to help somebody, help them like they matter.

1 — The Kitchen Before the Route

BROTHER BOXCAR: Soup ready.

GLITCHBOT: Statement premature. Temperature check required. Container check required. Allergen information required. Serving equipment required.

BROTHER BOXCAR: Robot... I said the soup ready.

GLITCHBOT: The soup may be ready. The mission is not.

ISAAC: He's right.

NARRATOR: Food outreach begins before anybody receives food. Preparation. Clean containers. Water. Spoons. Bags. Pamphlets kept dry. A route. A plan. And enough respect to make sure good intentions do not become somebody else's problem.

2 — Deorum Wants to Come

DEORUM: I'm coming.

ISAAC: Not this route.

DEORUM: Why?

ISAAC: Because we just learned this. Every mission does not belong to everybody.

AUTUMN: You are helping. Who's counting the bags? Who's checking the water?

DEORUM: Me.

ISAAC: Who's making sure Glitchbot doesn't accidentally print forty-seven thousand pamphlets?

GLITCHBOT: Printing capacity remains available.

NARRATOR: The child is still part of the mission. Just from the part of the map that belongs to him.

3 — Build the Loadout

GLITCHBOT: OUTREACH LOADOUT. Soup. Food bags. Water. Pamphlets. Resource cards. Route markers. Backup bags. Trash bags. Clean serving tools.

PHILLY JESUS: And faith.

GLITCHBOT: Faith does not fit in inventory slot.

ISAAC: Put patience in there too.

BROTHER BOXCAR: We're gonna upgrade this robot one day.

4 — Illz Willz Brings the Garden

ILLZ WILLZ: What came out the garden?

ISAAC: Greens. Herbs. Vegetables. Whatever can travel clean.

ILLZ WILLZ: Good. Because outreach shouldn't only happen after something goes wrong. A garden is prevention too.

NARRATOR: The game teaches supply: where food came from, who prepared it, how much exists, how far it can travel, how many people it can serve, and what needs replacing tomorrow.

5 — Enter the Sugar Fog

GLITCHBOT: Sugar haze increasing. Visibility reduced. Chaos loops detected.

ISAAC: Stay on route.

BROTHER BOXCAR: Soup don't need sightseeing.

NARRATOR: Drop point. Food table. Water station. Resource handoff. Exit. If something changes, the player checks the map. Not their ego.

6 — The First Bowl

COMMUNITY VOICE: What's this?

BROTHER BOXCAR: Soup.

COMMUNITY VOICE: How much?

BROTHER BOXCAR: Nothing.

NARRATOR: Food outreach is not pity. It is restoration and respect. A person is hungry. There is food. Start there.

7 — Philly Jesus and the Pamphlets

PHILLY JESUS: Places that might actually help. Food. Health. Housing. Libraries. Whatever's active on today's route.

GLITCHBOT: Resource information must be current before distribution.

ISAAC: Don't hand people dead phone numbers and call it help.

8 — Water Malone's Station

WATER MALONE: Shade. Visible. Clear route in and out. Nobody gotta walk through the whole line just to get water.

WATER MALONE: Water should never have an ego. It just goes where it's needed.

9 — Chaos Loop Number One

GLITCHBOT: Chaos loop detected. Primary objective unaffected. Continue route.

NARRATOR: Can you keep helping without being pulled into every distraction? Can you recognize something that requires a different responder?

ISAAC: Stay on mission.

10 — Chaos Loop Number Two

COMMUNITY VOICE 3: I need somewhere to go tonight.

ISAAC: Okay. That's not a soup problem. Check the resource cards.

NARRATOR: Soup solves hunger for a moment. Water solves thirst. A housing problem needs a housing route. A medical problem needs medical support. A safety emergency needs the right emergency response.

11 — The Spoon Becomes a Lesson

BROTHER BOXCAR: Another bowl.

GLITCHBOT: New spoon.

BROTHER BOXCAR: You're gonna make me throw you in the soup.

GLITCHBOT: Contamination event predicted. Mission failure.

NARRATOR: Feeding somebody safely is part of feeding them.

12 — Hope Doesn't Always Look Like Hope

COMMUNITY VOICE 4: Thank you.

NARRATOR: One person eats. One person gets water. One person gets a phone number. One person learns where the library is. Sometimes hope looks enormous. Sometimes hope enters with a spoon.

13 — Count What Actually Happened

GLITCHBOT: Mission report. Soup bowls distributed. Water bottles. Food bags. Pamphlets. Resource cards.

ISAAC: If we wanna do it again, we need to know what happened.

NARRATOR: Good work leaves evidence. Count what ran out, what was requested, what worked, and what needs improvement—not to turn people into numbers, but to make the help better.

14 — The Route Home

PHILLY JESUS: Good day.

ISAAC: Useful day.

GLITCHBOT: Activity does not automatically equal effectiveness.

15 — Deorum's Report

DEORUM: Hi. How many bowls?

AUTUMN: Did everybody get back safe?

ISAAC: Yeah.

AUTUMN: Then start there.

Ending

BROTHER BOXCAR: I brought soup.

PHILLY JESUS: I brought pamphlets.

WATER MALONE: I brought water.

ILLZ WILLZ: I brought the garden.

GLITCHBOT: I brought documentation.

DEORUM: What did I bring?

ISAAC: You helped get everybody ready. Don't act like preparation doesn't count.

NARRATOR: Restoration is still a victory condition. One bowl. One bottle. One useful card. One safe route. One person treated like a person. Then come back.
