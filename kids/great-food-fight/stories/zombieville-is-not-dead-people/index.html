Zombieville Is Not Dead People

Don’t Judge the Fog

NARRATOR

The map called it Zombieville. That name sounded funny until they got close. Then nobody laughed. Because there were no dead people walking around. No graves opening. No monsters hunting brains. There were people: tired people, hungry people, kids, elders, families, workers, people carrying things nobody could see. Above the neighborhood hung a sweet, heavy, sticky fog that made everything feel harder than it should.

1 — The Name Is Wrong If You Don’t Understand It

DEORUM

Why do they call it Zombieville?

ISAAC

That’s exactly why we gotta explain it right. The people aren’t zombies.

GLITCHBOT

Source definition: systems draining life.

ISAAC

Little by little. No fresh food nearby. Not enough money. Not enough rest. Too much stress. Bad information. Bad options. Too many problems happening at once. You wake up already tired, and then life charges you again.

Realm law: Do not confuse what happened to somebody with who somebody is.

2 — Life-Drain Vision

GLITCHBOT

New ability available: LIFE-DRAIN VISION.

It does not identify bad people. It identifies pressure: food-access gaps, exhaustion zones, long travel routes, resource shortages, unsafe shortcuts, despair fog, survival loops.

ISAAC

We don’t scan people. We scan problems.

3 — Don’t Judge the Fog

ISAAC

Because tired can come from a hundred places. Don’t guess somebody’s whole life because you saw five seconds of it.

GLITCHBOT

Mission rule activated: DON’T JUDGE THE FOG.

ISAAC

First: look. Listen. Learn. Then help where help actually helps.

4 — The Edge of the Map

AUTUMN

Where exactly are you going?

ISAAC

Not inside. Drop point.

Helping somebody does not mean dragging your family into every situation. We find the right person to carry the mission farther. You don’t have to personally do every part.

NARRATOR

This is another kind of strength: delegation, discernment, knowing where your mission ends and somebody else’s begins.

5 — Philly Jesus at the Handoff

PHILLY JESUS

Food bags? Pamphlets? Water? Good. Then y’all did your part. That’s my route. Your route ends here today.

NARRATOR

You don’t have to walk into every fire to serve somebody in the smoke.

ISAAC

Heroism with no judgment gets people hurt.

6 — Brother Boxcar’s Food Line

BROTHER BOXCAR

Soup coming through. Food bags on the left. Water on the right. Nobody gotta explain their whole life to eat.

NARRATOR

The mission asks: What do you need right now? Food. Water. Information. A route. A phone number. A place to sit. A next step.

7 — The Sugar Haze

ISAAC

Sometimes the game makes an invisible problem visible.

NARRATOR

The sugar haze is a game metaphor. Corner-store traps glow. Fresh-food gaps appear on the map. Exhaustion leaves footprints. Bad loops circle back on themselves. The game asks: Can this be repaired? Can somebody deliver something? Can we mark this location? Can we come back with better tools?

8 — Water Malone Doesn’t Fight Anybody

WATER MALONE

Every mission ain’t a boss battle. Sometimes the field just needs water.

NARRATOR

No crown ceremony. No battlefield. No giant attack. Just water moving where water is needed.

9 — Illz Willz and the Garden Answer

ILLZ WILLZ

Don’t build a rescue fantasy. Build something that can return next week. Nobody waters a garden once, then gets offended when it gets thirsty again.

10 — The Child Sees the Difference

DEORUM

So Zombieville doesn’t mean these are bad people. And we’re not saving everybody. We’re helping fix stuff.

ISAAC

Now you got it.

11 — Return With Information

NARRATOR

The mission ends with notes: locations, needs, routes, questions. Information is part of restoration.

12 — Back to the Garden

NARRATOR

The game does not keep the family in darkness because darkness happened. They return home. Back to the garden. Back to food. Back to routine. Back to each other.

DEORUM

Broccoli?

ISAAC

Broccoli.

13 — What Zombieville Really Means

NARRATOR

Zombieville is what happens in the game when systems begin taking more energy from people than they give back. The people are still people. The children are still children. The elders are still elders. The families are still families. The mission is not to judge them. The mission is to help life come back.

They were not monsters. They were people with their energy stolen.

GLITCHBOT

Mission complete. Do not judge the fog. Scan problems, not people. Food outreach requires dignity. Safety is part of service. Delegation is not abandonment. Information is a resource. Routine restores the player.

WATER MALONE

And bring water.

BROTHER BOXCAR

And soup.

PHILLY JESUS

And pamphlets.

ILLZ WILLZ

And come back.

ISAAC

That’s the important one. Come back.

NARRATOR

Because hope isn’t just showing up once. Hope builds a route.
