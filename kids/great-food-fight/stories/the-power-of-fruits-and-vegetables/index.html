Performance Script 005

The Power of Fruits and Vegetables

Food That Fights Back

Founder Approved • Performance Draft

This public reading copy was built from recovered Great Food Fight food-power mechanics and cleared by the Founder. It is a new performance draft, not an older verbatim recovered script.

Opening

NARRATOR: You have been lied to. Not about everything. Just about something very important. Somewhere along the way, fruits and vegetables got terrible marketing. Broccoli became punishment. Carrots became homework. Spinach became something somebody threatened you with before dessert. Fruit got chopped into little cups, and vegetables got pushed around plates like they owed somebody money. The Great Food Fight has another idea. What if food had swagger?

1 — The Table

DEORUM: So all of them got powers?

ISAAC: Different powers.

DEORUM: Based on the food?

ISAAC: Food. History. Personality. Culture. Story. What they survived. What they're good at.

GLITCHBOT: Classification: Edible superheroes with unresolved emotional issues.

ISAAC: That's disrespectful.

GLITCHBOT: Accuracy remains high.

2 — Water Malone

WATER MALONE: First thing you need to know about water: everybody needs it. King or farmer. Soldier or baby. Fruit or vegetable. Don't matter.

DEORUM: What's your power?

WATER MALONE: Flow. Hydration. Cleansing. Recovery. You get tired? I help the field breathe. You get stuck? Water finds another way. You poison the swamp? Now you got my attention.

GLITCHBOT: Water Malone classification: Support. Defense. Field restoration. Royal drip.

WATER MALONE: You almost had it.

3 — General Rich Broccoli

BROCCOLI: Everybody behind me.

DEORUM: Why?

BROCCOLI: Because that's where I'm strongest. Between danger and somebody I'm responsible for.

General Rich Broccoli is built around defense: armor, discipline, the Chloro Blade, battlefield awareness, and the memory of a garden that once needed protecting.

BROCCOLI: Strength ain't running toward every fight. Strength is knowing what you're protecting before you swing.

GLITCHBOT: Trauma discipline detected.

BROCCOLI: Don't diagnose me, toaster.

4 — Colonel Corn

COLONEL CORN: Hold the row! Left side steady! No kernel breaks formation!

DEORUM: He's serious.

ISAAC: Extremely.

COLONEL CORN: A harvest doesn't happen because one stalk felt inspirational. Rows. Timing. Work. Weather. Patience. Formation.

Colonel Corn understands abundance, but abundance requires structure. One seed can become many. One row can feed people. One field can become a responsibility.

5 — Corny

CORNY: Hold the line? Man, loosen the line up. Put some rhythm in it.

COLONEL CORN: Corny.

CORNY: Colonel.

COLONEL CORN: We are training.

CORNY: Exactly. Nobody said training had to sound miserable.

Corny reminds the game that joy is power too. Laughter can break tension. Rhythm can move a team. But when somebody feels unseen, even joy can become a wound.

6 — Captain Kane

CAPTAIN KANE: Roots remember.

DEORUM: What does that mean?

CAPTAIN KANE: Means you don't eat history without learning where it came from. Sugar ain't just sweetness. Cane got history. Labor got history. Land got history. People got history.

ISAAC: That's why he's Captain Kane.

CAPTAIN KANE: And that's why power without memory gets dangerous.

7 — Brainberry Clan

BRAINBERRY: You don't win every battle by being the loudest thing on the screen.

GLITCHBOT: Brainberry tactical map online. Enemy route calculated. Three safer paths found. One ridiculous path found.

DEORUM: What's the ridiculous one?

ISAAC: That's the one he's gonna pick.

Brainberry power is planning: observe, map, think, move. A strong team needs somebody who can see the whole board.

8 — Ginger and Garlic

GINGER NINJA: Quiet.

GARLIC NINJA: Quieter.

GINGER NINJA: Some problems announce themselves.

GARLIC NINJA: Others sneak in.

GINGER NINJA: So we sneak better.

Their powers are fictionalized stealth-combat mechanics inspired by the larger food-and-health theme—not a substitute for real medical care.

9 — Dragon Fruit

DRAGON FRUIT: You called?

ISAAC: Not yet.

DRAGON FRUIT: You were going to.

Dragon Fruit changes the shape of the battlefield: spike armor, sky breach, field reset. When the battle gets trapped in one pattern, Dragon Fruit breaks the pattern.

DRAGON FRUIT: Sometimes the whole field needs another chance.

10 — The Sky Defense

DEORUM: Was that fruit artillery?

GLITCHBOT: Confirmed. Pomegranate anti-air.

Pomegranate guards the air. Pineapple drops into dangerous heat with fireproof tactical armor. Blueberry moves where enemies cannot see. Fruit Force is not one power. Veggie Force is not one power. The point is combination.

11 — The Power Isn't Just Combat

ISAAC: Food is bigger than fighting.

Food is memory. Grandma's recipe. Dad cooking too much of something. A fruit you only saw when somebody came home from another country. The smell from a restaurant on the block. A garden where a kid sees something grow for the first time. A recipe somebody carried across an ocean. A seed somebody protected. A table somebody finally got everybody to sit around.

12 — Fruit Force

GLITCHBOT: Fruit Force!

FRUIT VOICES: READY!

Fruit Force moves. They adapt. They hit from weird angles. They bring color to the battlefield.

13 — Veggie Force

GLITCHBOT: Veggie Force!

VEGGIE VOICES: ROOTED!

Veggie Force holds the ground. Roots. Defense. Formation. Strength. Recovery. Field control. Sometimes the power isn't moving faster. Sometimes the power is refusing to be moved.

14 — Combo Mode

GLITCHBOT: TACTICAL FOOD COMBO AVAILABLE.

Water Malone plus Broccoli: Hydrated Defense.
Colonel Corn plus Brainberry: Harvest Formation.
Ginger plus Garlic: Germslayer Stealth.
Dragon Fruit plus Pomegranate: Sky Break Defense.
Corny plus music: Unpredictable.

15 — The Lesson

Nobody wins because one food is declared the greatest food on Earth. Different foods. Different places. Different families. Different cultures. Different strengths.

Ask instead: What is this? Where did it come from? Who eats it? How does it grow? How is it prepared? What does it mean to people? And what ridiculous superpower would it have if it could fight?

16 — The World Is Bigger Than This Garden

DEORUM: Are these all the fruits?

ISAAC: Not even close.

GLITCHBOT: Global produce database: Extremely disrespectful to attempt in one level.

ISAAC: Exactly. This garden ain't the whole planet. This is where we're starting.

Somewhere beyond Realm Philadelphia, other seeds are waiting. Other recipes. Other gardens. Other stories. Other foods children have not met yet. But that's another journey.

Ending

WATER MALONE: Flow.

BROCCOLI: Protect.

COLONEL CORN: Hold the row.

CORNY: Find the rhythm.

CAPTAIN KANE: Remember the roots.

BRAINBERRY: Think first.

GINGER NINJA: Move quietly.

GARLIC NINJA: Strike smart.

DRAGON FRUIT: Reset the field.

NARRATOR: The Power of Fruits and Vegetables. Not because food needs capes. Because children deserve a world interesting enough to make them curious.
