Performance Script 002

DWARVEN WORK BEFORE THE WONDER

FOUNDER APPROVED • 2026-10-01

Before Realm Philadelphia looks magical, somebody has to sweep, fix, measure, clean, carry, print and prepare the ground.

Opening

NARRATOR

Everybody wants to see the castle. Nobody asks who swept the floor.

Everybody wants the flying car. Nobody asks who found the wrench.

Everybody wants the magical city. Nobody asks who carried the trash bags down the steps.

Before the wonder... there was work.

Before Realm Philadelphia looked like a realm, somebody had to fix the building.

Scene 1 — The Morning After the Magic

GLITCHBOT

Good morning. Yesterday's results: Family Rhythm Aura unlocked. Unknown door detected. Adventure probability increasing.

ISAAC

Good. Grab the broom.

GLITCHBOT

Clarification requested. Should we not investigate the mysterious magical door?

ISAAC

Door ain't going nowhere. This trash is.

GLITCHBOT

Adventure postponed for sanitation.

ISAAC

Correct.

NARRATOR

That is the first law of Dwarven Territory. Magic does not cancel chores. Magic makes the chores more suspicious.

Scene 2 — Tools Have Homes

ISAAC

Hammer. Tape. Level. Drill. Pliers. Screwdriver. Where's my pencil?

GLITCHBOT

Searching.

ISAAC

Don't search the whole house. Search where the pencil is supposed to be.

GLITCHBOT

Concept detected: Organization.

ISAAC

Exactly. Everything needs a home. Your tools. Your books. Your files. Your ideas. Even your little robot self.

GLITCHBOT

My home is currently listed as: Wherever the charger is.

ISAAC

Accurate.

NARRATOR

A missing tool costs time. A messy workspace costs attention. And attention is one of the most expensive materials in the whole kingdom.

Scene 3 — Measure Twice Mode

ISAAC

Hold that end.

GLITCHBOT

Measurement ready.

ISAAC

What you got?

GLITCHBOT

Forty-eight inches.

ISAAC

Measure it again.

GLITCHBOT

Forty-eight inches.

ISAAC

Good. Now we cut.

GLITCHBOT

Why measure twice if the first number was correct?

ISAAC

Because sometimes the first number ain't correct. And drywall don't care about your confidence.

GLITCHBOT

New rule logged. Confidence is not a measuring device.

NARRATOR

Rush the job and the pieces stop fitting. Slow down, check the work, and suddenly the wall starts becoming a wall again.

Scene 4 — Brother Splinter

BROTHER SPLINTER

Hold up. Don't throw that away yet.

ISAAC

What you seeing?

BROTHER SPLINTER

Two good pieces inside one ugly piece.

GLITCHBOT

Material classified as scrap.

BROTHER SPLINTER

That's because you looking at what it was. I'm looking at what it can still become.

GLITCHBOT

Updating definition. Scrap: Material awaiting a better question.

Scene 5 — The Dwarf Selling Copper

DWARF SELLING COPPER

Copper! Good copper! Old pipe! Wire! Little bit bent, still got value!

GLITCHBOT

Unusual merchant detected.

ISAAC

Welcome to Dwarven Territory.

DWARF SELLING COPPER

Metal got memory. Building got memory. Every repair tells you what somebody did before you got there.

GLITCHBOT

Can copper speak?

DWARF SELLING COPPER

Only if you know how to listen with a multimeter.

NARRATOR

Basements become mines. Toolboxes become treasure chests. Copper pipe becomes Dwarven gold. Tradespeople become keepers of practical magic.

Scene 6 — Printer Lab

GLITCHBOT

Printing initiated.

ISAAC

Watch the paper.

GLITCHBOT

Why?

ISAAC

Because machines don't care if you're distracted.

GLITCHBOT

Paper jam detected.

ISAAC

See? Clear it. Reload it. Run it again.

NARRATOR

A book does not become a book because somebody imagined it. Somebody formats it. Prints it. Cuts it. Binds it. Checks it. Fixes the mistake. And runs it again.

Scene 7 — The Dwarven Queen of Shortcuts

DWARVEN QUEEN OF SHORTCUTS

Why are you doing all that? Just cover it.

ISAAC

Cover what?

DWARVEN QUEEN OF SHORTCUTS

The problem. Put something pretty over it. Nobody will know.

GLITCHBOT

Shortcut proposal detected. Estimated time saved: Twenty-two minutes.

ISAAC

Estimated time lost later?

GLITCHBOT

Unknown. Possibly catastrophic.

DWARVEN QUEEN OF SHORTCUTS

Catastrophic is such an ugly word. I prefer... future surprise.

ISAAC

Nah. Open it back up. Find the problem. Fix the problem. Then close it.

Done-looking is not the same as done.

Scene 8 — Clean the Ground

GLITCHBOT

We repaired the wall. Why are we cleaning again?

ISAAC

Because the job isn't done while somebody can trip over the job. And because tomorrow-me shouldn't have to fight yesterday-me.

NARRATOR

Sweep. Bag. Sort. Carry. Stack. Put the tools back. Clear the walkway. Charge the batteries. Check the plugs. Prepare the next station.

It does not look magical. That is why people miss the magic.

Scene 9 — Driving Through Dwarven Territory

NARRATOR

Construction vans. Basement doors. Copper. Ladders. Work boots. Dumpsters. Scaffolding. Old bricks. New wire. Crews rebuilding things most people will never think about again once they work.

GLITCHBOT

Realm signal detected. Dwarven Territory: tools, trades, repairs, copper, basements, construction.

ISAAC

See? Philadelphia been magical. People just got used to looking at it.

Scene 10 — The Prep Badge

NARRATOR

The room is cleaner. The wall is stronger. The tools are where they belong. The printer is ready. The trash is out. The measurements are checked.

Nothing flew. Nothing exploded. Nobody became king.

And yet the player did something important.

The player prepared.

GLITCHBOT

Mission analysis: Sweep. Fix. Measure. Clean. Carry. Print. Prepare.

ISAAC

That's how big dreams start.

GLITCHBOT

Correction. Big dreams started earlier. This appears to be how big dreams survive.
